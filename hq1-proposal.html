Executive Summary

Inquiring Minds Group (IMG) is a St. Louis-based operator seeking to lease and activate 1724 N. 13th Street as a long-term headquarters for storage, fulfillment, creator support, and business services. The ask is designed to reduce vacancy risk for ownership while IMG phases into stable occupancy.

St. Louis is the natural location for HQ-1 because it sits between IMG’s existing relationships in Chicago, Kansas City, Dallas-Fort Worth, and New Orleans, and founder leadership maintains longstanding ties to the region, making it a practical base for storage, coordination, and operations.

We are not asking the owner to underwrite a speculative redevelopment story. We are asking for a practical lease structure that gives the building daily use, a responsible occupant, and a clear path to full rent.

  • 8,910 SF footprint for phased occupancy.
  • Loading docks for receiving, shipping, and fulfillment.
  • Office area for HQ administration and client intake.

Why Immediate Occupancy Matters Now

  • Property gets active stewardship and daily presence.
  • Vacancy exposure and deterioration are reduced.
  • Phased activation lowers startup failure risk.
  • Long-term tenant focus supports continuity instead of turnover.
  • Ownership gets a path to active use instead of empty space.
  • What ownership gets: a deposit, daily stewardship, phased activation, and a tenant building toward long-term occupancy.
  • What we are not asking for: major construction before the building is occupied or rent based on speculation.
  • What we are offering: a realistic path to active use, stabilized operations, and eventual full-market rent.

Lease Request

We present the following requested lease structure as a partnership approach to property activation. The terms are designed to reduce vacancy risk while allowing IMG to invest in cleanup, organization, and operational revenue before reaching full rent obligations.

The phased structure gives IMG time to organize product inventory, activation equipment, packing workflows, storage clients, rental systems, and business-support operations before full rent begins.

Requested Lease Structure

Term Requested Structure
Lease Duration Five (5) years
Security Deposit $5,000
Months 1-6 Rent abatement (no rent due)
Months 7-12 50% of full market rent
Year 2-5 Full market rent (to be negotiated)

Why This Structure Works

The abatement and reduced-rate periods are not designed to shift risk to the owner. They give IMG enough runway to clean up the property, establish operating routines, and build revenue without asking for a construction-heavy start. The owner gets a deposit, phased occupancy, and a clear activation plan.

  • Deposit and phased occupancy reduce vacancy risk.
  • Five-year term supports continuity instead of turnover.
  • No major construction is required before occupancy begins.

At that point, full market rent is appropriate and sustainable. The proposal is intentionally conservative: it asks for time to stabilize the operation, not for the owner to underwrite speculative development.

Owner Protections

  • Security deposit held against damages or default.
  • Personal guarantee from principal operator.
  • Regular property inspections and maintenance compliance.
  • Insurance requirements in place before occupancy.
  • Right to terminate for material breach.
