Executive Summary

Inquiring Minds Group (IMG) is a St. Louis-based business operator seeking to lease and activate the property at 1724 N. 13th Street. We intend to occupy, maintain, and grow within this property for the long term.

IMG operates multiple business initiatives across storage, fulfillment, creator support, and business services. We require approximately 5,000–8,000 square feet of warehouse and flex space to consolidate operations, support regional partners, and establish a permanent operational headquarters.

We propose a five-year lease structure with phased rent escalation designed to support property activation while demonstrating long-term commitment. Our approach prioritizes immediate occupancy, gradual build-out, and sustainable growth rather than speculative development.

We selected this address because the current building footprint and access points support the way IMG actually operates. The proposal is built around the property that exists, not around a speculative redevelopment story.

Why Occupancy Matters Now

This section addresses the owner's perspective directly. Vacant commercial property carries costs and risks that an occupied building does not. Our proposal is designed to reduce those costs and risks from the first day of tenancy.

Why Immediate Occupancy Benefits Ownership

An occupied building is a maintained building. A vacant building is a deteriorating asset. The difference is not theoretical — it is visible, measurable, and cumulative.

Active Stewardship

  • Property receives active stewardship: Daily presence means regular maintenance, prompt attention to issues, and immediate reporting of problems before they become costly repairs
  • Building receives daily presence: Lights, activity, and regular foot traffic reduce the appearance of abandonment that attracts unwanted attention

Reduced Vacancy Exposure

  • Reduced vacancy exposure: Each month of vacancy carries holding costs — taxes, insurance, maintenance, and opportunity cost. Immediate occupancy stops that accumulation
  • Reduced vandalism risk: An occupied property with regular activity is less attractive to vandalism and break-in than a vacant, unmonitored building
  • Reduced deterioration associated with long-term vacancy: Vacant buildings deteriorate faster due to lack of climate control monitoring, undetected leaks, pest intrusion, and deferred maintenance. Active occupancy slows or prevents this cycle

Why a Long-Term Tenant Is Preferable

  • Long-term tenant pursuing growth rather than temporary occupancy: IMG is not seeking short-term space while searching for a better option. We are consolidating multiple business lines into a permanent headquarters
  • Phased activation reduces startup failure risk: Our plan begins with low-cost, low-risk occupancy and expands only as operations stabilize. This reduces the likelihood of a tenant who overextends and vacates early

The owner's choice is not between IMG and a perfect tenant. It is between IMG and continued vacancy, with the costs and risks vacancy entails. We offer a realistic, executable path to sustained occupancy.

Lease Request

We present two lease structures as a partnership approach to property activation. Either structure is designed to reduce vacancy risk for the owner while allowing IMG to invest in cleanup, organization, and operational revenue before reaching full rent obligations. The owner can accept either option.

Option A: Extended Ramp-Up (Preferred)

Term Proposed Structure
Lease Duration Five (5) years
Security Deposit $5,000
Months 1–6 Rent abatement (no rent due)
Months 7–12 50% of full market rent
Year 2–5 Full market rent (to be negotiated)
Sublease Rights Permitted subject to owner approval of subtenants
Renewal Option One five-year renewal at market rate

Rationale: Longer abatement period allows more time to build revenue before full rent obligations begin. Lower deposit preserves IMG capital for cleanup and initial operations.

Why This Structure Works

The abatement and reduced-rate periods are not designed to shift risk to the owner. They are designed to give IMG enough runway to clean up the property, establish operating routines, and build revenue without asking for a construction-heavy start. In practical terms, the owner gets a committed tenant with a deposit, phased occupancy, and a clear activation plan.

Insurance & Property Responsibility

IMG will maintain general liability insurance naming the property owner as additional insured. IMG accepts responsibility for interior maintenance, utilities, janitorial, and day-to-day property care. Triple-net (NNN) obligations — including property taxes, building insurance, and structural maintenance — will be negotiated as a separately documented addendum to the lease.

IMG's objective is for the property to have:

  • An established tenant with operational history
  • Active occupancy and regular activity
  • Documented maintenance and stewardship
  • Demonstrated ability to meet financial obligations

At that point, full market rent is appropriate and sustainable. The proposal is intentionally conservative: it asks for time to stabilize the operation, not for the owner to underwrite speculative development.

Owner Protections (Both Options)

  • Insurance: General liability naming owner as additional insured; property coverage for interior contents and operations
